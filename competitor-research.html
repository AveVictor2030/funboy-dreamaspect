How this report is built
  • Public pages only — logged-out Instagram and TikTok profiles. No account login, no native Insights, no private dashboards.
  • Counts must be visible on the public post or profile. If likes or views are hidden, the field stays blank and that post is excluded from engagement averages.
  • Instagram reel views use public video_view_count, not Insights reach or impressions.
  • Window: first 12-tile Instagram grid on @funboy, Pacific dates from taken_at. TikTok post rows only when the logged-out video grid loads.
  • Collected November 3, 2026. Window October 10–November 1, 2026.
